# 🪲 Beetles AI - Product Roadmap

## Positioning
**"Verify before you publish. Know why, not just what."**

The forensic verification tool built for journalists,
not developers.

## Target User
- Independent journalists
- Small-to-medium newsrooms
- Fact-checking organizations
- Content moderators
- PR agencies

## Market Gap We Address
Existing tools (Hive, Optic, etc.) offer:
- ❌ Single-model detection (unreliable)
- ❌ Score without explanation
- ❌ Developer-focused (not workflow-friendly)
- ❌ No actionable recommendations

Beetles AI offers:
- ✅ Multi-model consensus detection
- ✅ Explainable results with reasoning
- ✅ Journalist-friendly workflow
- ✅ Actionable "Shareability Verdict"
